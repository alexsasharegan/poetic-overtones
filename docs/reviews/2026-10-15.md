## 4. Risks I know of

* **Renderer tests are manual.** The painter and the app were checked with ad hoc headless-browser scripts, which are not in `npm test`. A small browser test harness would protect them.
* **Speed on long texts.** Every edit takes a full snapshot (for undo) and repaints everything. Fine for poems; too slow near the 1,000,000 code point limit of W-04.
* **Two loaders to keep in step.** `check.py` and `src/core/load.js` must agree, rule for rule (AGENTS.md says so).
* **Accessibility.** Added characters are hidden from screen readers; selection needs a mouse or touch.
* **Older prototypes.** `prototypes/layers/` and `scansion.html` duplicate parts of `src/`; they could be retired once the app covers them.
